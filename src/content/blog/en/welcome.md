This is the start of my personal blog. I'll use it to collect experiences,
small discoveries and things I'd like to explore further.

## A simple starting point

The blog is built with [Astro](https://astro.build/), and the posts are written
in Markdown. That's enough for what I need: text, links and a little code.

```ts
const message: string = 'Hello, world!';
console.log(message);
```

The goal is to have a place to write and share, without making publishing
more complicated than it needs to be.
